# Student Journey — Project Overview

> A one-page explainer: what this platform is, the problem it solves, and what it does.

## In one line

**An EdTech platform that builds each learner a personalized path — from "tell us about
yourself" all the way to AI-curated study material, auto-generated quizzes, structured
courses with certificates, and college-decision tools — all adapting to who they are and
where they are in their education.**

---

## The problem it solves (the motive)

Learning today is **fragmented and generic**:

- **Content isn't matched to the person.** A 10th-grader, a 3rd-year engineering student,
  a postgrad researcher, and a working professional all get the same one-size-fits-all
  material — even though their goals, exams, and context are completely different.
- **Learners juggle too many disconnected tools** — one site to find study content,
  another for practice tests, another for courses, another to figure out which college
  their exam rank can get them. Nothing talks to each other.
- **Self-learners lack structure and feedback.** It's hard to know *what* to study,
  *whether you're improving*, and *what to do next*.

**Student Journey collapses all of that into one guided, personalized journey** where every
step feeds the next.

---

## The solution (how it works)

The platform learns who the learner is, then personalizes everything downstream from that
profile:

1. **Onboarding that adapts to you.** Sign up and tell us your type — **student** or
   **working professional**. For students it goes further and adapts to your **education
   level**: a high-schooler is asked about streams, target exams (JEE/NEET) and dream
   colleges; an undergrad about their degree and year; a postgrad about their specialization
   and research phase. The questions fit *your* reality.
2. **AI builds your study material.** Your profile + preferences are turned into smart web
   searches, and an AI curates the results — deduping, ranking, summarizing, and tagging the
   best articles **and** videos. Save favorites to a personal library with semantic search.
3. **AI tests you and tracks progress.** It generates quizzes grounded in what you've
   actually studied, scores them instantly with explanations, and shows per-topic strengths
   and weaknesses.
4. **Structured courses & certificates.** Enrol in full courses, work through lessons, track
   progress, and earn a verifiable certificate — and the course syllabus automatically powers
   your study material and quizzes.
5. **College-decision tools built in.** Deep links to the organization's **Course Finder**
   (discover programs/colleges) and **Cutoff Predictor** (enter your rank → see which colleges
   you can realistically get), surfaced contextually for students targeting entrance exams.

---

## Key features

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🧭 **Adaptive onboarding** | Branches by user type, and *further* by student education level — questions fit the learner |
| 📚 **AI study material** | Personalized search + LLM curation into ranked articles & videos; save-to-library with semantic search (RAG) |
| 📝 **AI quizzes & tests** | Auto-generated MCQs grounded in your material, instant scoring, explanations, per-topic analytics |
| 🎓 **Courses & certifications** | Structured course → module → lesson, progress tracking, auto-issued verifiable certificates |
| 🔗 **Connected ecosystem** | Course Finder + Cutoff Predictor (college decisions) woven into the journey |
| 🔁 **Everything feeds everything** | Profile drives content; courses drive quizzes; results drive what's next |
| 🛠️ **Admin authoring** | Internal team can publish courses — the same path a future bulk data-import will use |

*On the roadmap: an email/reminder engine, formal full-length mock tests, and a separate
conversational mock-interview agent.*
